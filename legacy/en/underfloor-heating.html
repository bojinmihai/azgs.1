    As a finishing and installation company in the Utrecht region, we install underfloor heating in homes for both new construction and renovations. With careful planning and installation, we deliver even heat distribution in your home in and around Woerden and Utrecht.

    What we provide

    We handle different types of underfloor heating in the Utrecht region:

    • Water-based underfloor heating systems in living areas

    How we work

    From assessment to balancing

    Four steps to carefully install your underfloor heating.

    1

    Assessment of floor and installation

    We start with an assessment of the existing or planned floor buildup and your heating system. We review the available height, the type of floor finish, and the rooms where underfloor heating is wanted. Based on that, we advise which system is the best fit.

    2

    Plan and circuit layout

    Next, we create a layout plan for the underfloor heating, focusing on heat distribution and the position of circuits and piping. We account for walkways, furniture, and heat loss along exterior walls, so heat is distributed as evenly as possible.

    3

    Installing the system

    We then lay the piping or electric mats according to the plan. We secure the systems properly to the subfloor and account for expansion gaps and the final floor finish. Connections to the existing heating system we coordinate carefully.

    4

    Balancing and walkthrough

    After installation, the system is brought online and balanced further as needed. We explain how best to use the underfloor heating and how to set it for comfortable, efficient operation. That way, you can confidently switch to a different way of heating your home.

    Materials and quality

    We work with underfloor heating systems and materials from reliable suppliers. We match the choice between water-based and electric to the room, floor finish, and your preferences. By installing carefully and thoroughly checking connections, we deliver a durable system that works well with your existing installation and floor.

    Frequently asked questions

    Common questions about underfloor heating

    Is underfloor heating suitable for my existing home?

    In many existing homes, underfloor heating works well, provided the floor buildup and height allow it. For renovations, we look at each room to determine what's possible and what adjustments are needed. We discuss this thoroughly up front during a visit in the Utrecht region.

    Can underfloor heating be combined with a wood floor?

    Yes, underfloor heating can often be combined with certain types of wood floors or parquet. What matters is that the materials are suitable for the combination. We advise you, together with your flooring supplier, on suitable products and buildup.

    What's the difference between water-based and electric underfloor heating?

    Water-based underfloor heating works with warm water running through pipes, while electric underfloor heating works through electric mats or cables. Water-based systems are often used in larger rooms; electric systems are more common in smaller rooms or for specific applications. We'll help you decide which fits best.

    How long does underfloor heating installation take?

    The duration depends on the number of rooms, the system chosen, and the adjustments needed to the floor. Small projects can be done quickly; larger renovations take more days. You get a timeline estimate up front for your home in the Utrecht region.

    Do you work as installer only, without further finishing?

    Yes, we can install just the underfloor heating, but we can also coordinate the rest of the finishing, such as the floor covering. We tailor this to your preferences and the parties involved. That way, it stays clear who is responsible for what.
