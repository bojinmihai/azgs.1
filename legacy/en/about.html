    About us

    Finishing and installation company in the Utrecht region for your home

    AZ Grand Solutions is a finishing and installation company serving homeowners in the Utrecht region, with over 35 years of international experience. We combine clean finishing with reliable technical installations, so your home looks good and works well.

    What we stand for

    Five principles that show up in every project

    At AZ Grand Solutions, it's about craftsmanship, honest work, and clean finishing. Clear agreements, careful with your home, and you always know where you stand.

    Direct personal phone contact

    When you call, you get direct personal contact with someone who listens to your situation and brings in the right specialist. No long menus and no unnecessary transfers between departments — just a clear explanation and an honest assessment of what's needed.

    Respect for your home

    We treat your home with respect, as if we were working in our own house. That means we work carefully, protect floors and furniture where needed, and handle your belongings properly. You experience as little disruption as possible during the work.

    A commitment is a commitment

    We take scheduling and commitments seriously. Up front, we agree clearly on what we'll do, when we'll come, and roughly how long it takes. During the work, we don't leave jobs half-done. If something unexpected comes up, we talk it through with you first.

    Delivered cleanly

    For us, a project is only truly finished when it's been delivered cleanly. We clean up materials and waste as much as possible, leave the work area in your home tidy, and walk through the result together with you. You're not left with a mess or unfinished corners.

    Transparent pricing

    We aim for transparent pricing so you understand up front how the costs break down. We explain what work is needed, what's included, and what optional extras exist. No hidden costs — an honest explanation, without unpleasant surprises afterward.

    The Utrecht Region

    AZ Grand Solutions works primarily for homeowners in the Utrecht region, with Woerden as our base. Within a radius of about 60 kilometers, we handle finishing and installations in places like Utrecht, Amersfoort, Nieuwegein, Zeist, Houten, IJsselstein, Gouda, Alphen aan den Rijn, Hilversum, Veenendaal, Bunnik, and Woerden itself. Focusing on this region lets us plan reliably, keep travel times short, and stay on top of projects.
