How we work

From inspection to delivery

Four steps to carefully complete your interior painting project.

1

Inspection and color selection

We start with an inspection of the existing surfaces and discuss your preferences for colors and sheens. We look for damage, old paint layers, and any moisture spots so we know exactly what prep is needed for durable paintwork.

2

Surface prep

Next, we prep the surfaces carefully. We fill cracks and holes, sand existing layers where needed, and mask floors and furniture properly. This step is key to a clean finish and good adhesion of the new paint.

3

Painting with the right products

We then apply the paint in one or more coats, depending on the situation. We use the right products for walls, ceilings, and woodwork, with attention to quality and longevity. Working calmly and precisely, we deliver a clean, even result.

4

Delivery and follow-up

After painting, we clean up the work area and remove masking materials. Together with you, we walk through the rooms and review the final result. Any small touch-ups we handle on the spot, so you can enjoy the refreshed paint with confidence.

Materials and quality

For interior painting, we use quality paint and tools suited to the intensive use homes see. We match the type of paint to the room — for example, extra-scrubbable paint in kitchens or hallways. By working carefully and choosing the right products, we deliver a durable coat with a clean finish that stays looking good longer.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about painting

Do you paint single rooms?

Yes, we handle both smaller jobs — a single bedroom or living room — and larger multi-room projects. When you first get in touch, we discuss what exactly needs painting and set up a plan that fits your home in the Utrecht region.

Do I need to empty the room before painting?

We ask you to move smaller items out of the way, but larger furniture can usually stay in the room. We cover it properly and move it where needed. That way we work efficiently without unnecessary damage or paint splatters.

What paint do you use?

We use quality paint suited to interior use, matched to the surface and how the room gets used. Wall paint for walls and ceilings, trim paint for woodwork. During the on-site visit, we advise on the right products and possible color combinations.

How long does interior paint last?

The lifespan of interior paint depends on use, light exposure, and ventilation. Under normal conditions, well-done paintwork can look good for several years. We're happy to advise on maintenance and touching up higher-wear areas over time.

Do you work across all of the Netherlands?

No, we focus on the Utrecht region, within roughly a 60-kilometer radius of Woerden. That keeps travel times short and lets us plan and run projects more carefully. If you live in or around Utrecht, Amersfoort, Nieuwegein, Zeist, or one of the surrounding towns, we can usually help.
