How we work

From assessment to delivery

Four steps to carefully complete your heating project.

1

System assessment

We start with an assessment of your existing heating system and the changes you're planning. We inspect the radiators, lines, manifolds, and the connection to your heating system. That gives us a complete picture of what's needed.

2

Plan for radiators and piping

Next, we create a plan for installing or replacing radiators and for running or replacing piping. We determine the right radiator sizes, the routing for the pipes, and the position of manifolds and thermostats, matched to how the rooms are laid out and used.

3

Carrying out the work

We then carry out the work step by step. We install the radiators, run or replace piping, and mount manifolds and thermostatic valves. We check every connection carefully for tightness and finish everything cleanly.

4

Testing, bleeding, and walkthrough

After the work, we test the system, bleed the radiators, and balance where needed. We explain what was changed and how best to use the system. That way, you know exactly how your heating works and end up with a comfortable, well-functioning installation.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about heating

Can you replace or relocate my radiators?

Yes, we replace outdated radiators and can relocate them when the layout of your home changes. During the on-site visit, we assess which radiator sizes fit the room and discuss whether the piping needs to be adjusted for your home in the Utrecht region.

I have a leak in my heating line — can you repair it?

Yes, we repair leaks in heating lines. We trace the cause, repair or replace the damaged section, and check the tightness of the entire run. For acute leaks, you can also call our 24/7 emergency service.

Can you add extra radiators or connection points?

Yes, we can extend the system with extra radiators or connection points — for example, during an addition or a new room. We evaluate whether the existing system has enough capacity and which pipe route is best, taking walls, ceilings, and finishes into account.

Do you also install underfloor heating?

Yes, alongside classic radiator-based heating, we also install underfloor heating — water-based or electric. We can combine both systems or do them separately. More information on our underfloor heating page.

Where do you handle heating work?

We work within roughly a 60-kilometer radius of Woerden. Beyond Utrecht, we're active in Amersfoort, Nieuwegein, Zeist, Houten, IJsselstein, Gouda, Alphen aan den Rijn, Hilversum, Veenendaal, and Bunnik, among others. That lets us handle heating work across a large part of the Utrecht region.
