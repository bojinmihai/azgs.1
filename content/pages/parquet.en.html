How we work

From assessment to delivery

Four steps to carefully install your hardwood floor.

1

Site visit and floor type

We start with a site visit to look at the existing floor buildup. We discuss which type of parquet or hardwood floor you want — engineered plank, herringbone — and review the technical side: height, subfloor, and transitions to other rooms.

2

Subfloor prep

A proper subfloor is key to a stable hardwood floor. We level where needed, install a suitable subfloor layer, and account for movement and sound. That gives us a solid base for the parquet planks to go down cleanly.

3

Laying and finishing the parquet

Next, we lay the parquet in the chosen pattern, with attention to alignment and cutting around corners and door frames. We deliver clean joins and a calm, consistent layout. We install or restore baseboards around the perimeter so the floor is framed in neatly.

4

Delivery and care tips

Once the floor is installed, we clean up the work area and walk through the result with you. We explain how to use and care for your hardwood floor — cleaning, scratch protection — so you can enjoy your new parquet for as long as possible.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about parquet

Can you remove my old floor?

Yes, if needed we can remove existing flooring — carpet, laminate, old planks — before laying the parquet. During the on-site visit, we discuss what needs to go and how to handle disposal cleanly at your home in the Utrecht region.

Is parquet suitable for underfloor heating?

Many engineered-plank parquet floors work well with underfloor heating, provided the products and buildup are chosen correctly. We'll advise on suitable floor types and follow the manufacturer's guidelines and your heating system's layout during installation.

How long does parquet installation take?

The duration depends on the area, the pattern (straight plank or herringbone), and the state of the subfloor. Smaller rooms can be laid quickly; larger projects take more days. We give you a realistic timeline up front for your home in the Utrecht region.

Do you supply the parquet yourself?

We can work either with planks you supply or help you choose and order parquet from suitable suppliers. Either way, we pay attention to quality and fit for the situation, so the combination of material and installation works.

Do you work outside Utrecht city?

Yes, we work within roughly a 60-kilometer radius of Woerden. Beyond Utrecht, we're active in Amersfoort, Nieuwegein, Zeist, Houten, IJsselstein, Gouda, Alphen aan den Rijn, Hilversum, Veenendaal, and Bunnik, among others. That lets us lay parquet across a large part of the Utrecht region.
