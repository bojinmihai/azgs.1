Our services

Everything for finishing and installation, under one roof

Eight core services, one point of contact. Choose the service that fits your project, or request a complete solution.

Finishing

Drywall

We install drywall for clean, flat walls and ceilings in your home. From building metal-stud framing and insulation to seamless finishing — we deliver a level substrate ready for painting or tiling. Suitable for both renovations and new construction.

Finishing

Painting

We handle interior painting for walls, ceilings, and woodwork, with a clean, even finish. We carefully prep the surface — filling, sanding, and masking where needed — and then apply durable paint. From a single room to a full interior renovation.

Finishing

Parquet

We install parquet and hardwood floors for a durable, warm look in your home. Whether engineered planks, a classic herringbone pattern, or straight-plank layout — we deliver a stable subfloor, careful placement, and clean finishing around baseboards and doors. Suitable for renovations and new construction.

Finishing

Tiling

We handle tiling for bathrooms, kitchens, and restrooms, with precise wall- and floor-tile installation. We level the substrate, apply watertight sealing, and set the tiles with clean grout lines and proper drainage. Suitable for complete bathroom renovations or tiling specific areas.

Installations

Plumbing

We install plumbing for bathrooms and kitchens — toilets, sinks, showers, tubs, and faucets. Whether running new connections, replacing existing piping, or completing a full renovation: we work with reliable materials and make sure everything is delivered watertight and fully functional.

Installations

Heating

We run heating lines, install and replace radiators, and deliver a heating system that works reliably throughout your home. From individual radiators to complete piping runs — we work to current Dutch standards and finish neatly.

Installations

Underfloor Heating

We install underfloor heating for even, comfortable heat distribution throughout your home. We install both water-based and electric systems, matched to the room, floor buildup, and type of finish. Suitable for both new construction and renovations, including connecting to an existing heating system.

Installations

Electrical

We handle electrical work for your home: electrical panels, breaker boxes, wiring, outlets, switches, and light fixtures. From small additions to complete renovations — we work cleanly and safely, from the main panel to every endpoint, and deliver a tidy installation that meets current Dutch standards.
