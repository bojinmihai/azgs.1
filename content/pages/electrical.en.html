How we work

From assessment to delivery

Four steps to carefully complete your electrical project.

1

Assessment of the installation

We start with an assessment of your current electrical system and the changes you're planning. We inspect the electrical panel, existing circuits, and wiring, and discuss where you need additional outlets or modifications. This gives us a complete picture of the situation.

2

Plan for circuits and wiring

Next, we create a plan for expanding or modifying the system. We determine which circuits are needed, how wiring should be routed, and where outlets, switches, and light fixtures should go. We match the plan to how you use the space and the layout of each room.

3

Carrying out the work

We then carry out the electrical work step by step. We keep the panel organized and run wiring and cables cleanly. Outlets, switches, and light fixtures are installed carefully, with attention to safe connections and a tidy finish.

4

Testing and walkthrough

After the work, we test the system and verify that every circuit and endpoint works correctly. We explain what was changed and how the circuits are organized in the panel. That way, you know exactly how your system is laid out.

Materials and quality

For electrical work, we use reliable components for electrical panels, breaker boxes, and switchgear. We pay attention to appropriate wire gauges, correct breakers, and a clean layout. By working carefully and thoroughly checking every connection, we help deliver a safe electrical system with a clean finish in your home.

A clear point plan avoids unnecessary cutting and repair during renovation. Indicate where appliances, lighting, switches, and outlets will be located and which surfaces still need finishing. We then assess routes and required circuits.

  • Positions and quantities of outlets, switches, and lighting points

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about electrical work

Can you replace or expand my electrical panel?

Yes, we replace outdated electrical panels and can expand the breaker box if you need more circuits — for example, for kitchen appliances or other equipment. During the on-site visit, we assess what is technically necessary and sensible for your home in the Utrecht region.

Do you add outlets in existing rooms?

Yes, we can add extra outlets and switches to existing rooms. We evaluate on-site which wiring routes are possible and how to run them cleanly, taking walls, ceilings, and finishes into account.

Do you handle small electrical jobs?

We handle both larger projects and smaller modifications — such as adding a light fixture or a few outlets. When you first get in touch, we'll explain how and when we can schedule the work.

Do you coordinate with other contractors?

Yes, on remodels and renovations we coordinate our electrical work with other trades. That way, chases, wiring, and connections are completed at the right moment and line up with the rest of the work in your home.
