What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that your browser stores on your computer, tablet, or smartphone when you visit a website. They make it possible, for example, to display maps, remember settings, or ensure parts of a website work properly.

Alongside cookies, there are similar techniques such as scripts and pixels. For readability, this cookie policy uses "cookies" as a collective term for all techniques that can place or read information on your device, to the extent they fall under the cookie rules and the GDPR.

Does this website use cookies?

The AZ Grand Solutions (AZGS) website uses a limited number of cookies. We distinguish between functional cookies (which are always placed because they are needed for the website to work) and analytical Google Analytics cookies (which are only placed if you give consent via our cookie banner). We do not place marketing cookies and do not do profiling or remarketing.

Cookies may also be placed by third parties when you use certain parts of the website — such as clicking a WhatsApp link or submitting the contact form via Formspree. The website fonts are delivered locally from azgs.nl.

Overview of cookies on this website

On this website, cookies or similar techniques are only used by external services that load on specific pages or for certain actions. An overview follows below.

Google Analytics 4 (analytical, consent only)

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-DK6FZHQRCB) to create aggregated usage statistics, such as viewed pages and conversion actions. Measured actions include starting and successfully sending the contact form, choosing a request type or service, clicking phone, WhatsApp or email links, and downloading AZGS documents. After consent, Google Analytics places cookies such as _ga and _ga_<measurement-id>, which can remain for up to 2 years by default.

We place these cookies only if you have given consent via our cookie banner. Until you give consent, Google Analytics is not loaded: no Google Analytics cookies are placed and analytics measurement does not start. Only after you click "Accept" are the cookies placed and measurement begins.

We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, address or postcode data, free-text form entries, or other form contents to Google Analytics. URL queries and fragments are removed from the measured page location. Campaign and referral information is processed only as fixed categories; free-form UTM values and complete external referrer URLs are not sent to GA4. After consent, the content language, request type, service, a fixed B2B sector category, and safe internal paths for the entry page and CTA origin may also be measured.

Additional privacy settings in the website code: IP anonymization is enabled, Google Signals and ad personalization are disabled, and advertising consent remains denied. During the account review on 3 September 2026, Google Signals was disabled, email-address redaction was active, and event and user data retention was set to 14 months. Google Ads and remarketing are not part of this website implementation. The account and international-transfer settings are reviewed periodically and must remain documented in accordance with the actual situation.

Local browser preference (azgs-consent-v1)

To remember your cookie choice, we store the preference azgs-consent-v1 locally in your browser. It contains only the value "accepted" (you gave consent) or "rejected" (you refused). This is technically necessary to respect your choice on a next visit and therefore falls under functional storage for which no separate consent is required.

Session storage after consent

Only after you accept analytics, the website uses azgs-attribution-v1, azgs-entry-page-v1, and azgs-cta-origin-v1 in your browser's session storage. They contain only fixed source and medium categories and safe internal paths without a query or fragment. They are used to measure successive page views and conversion actions coherently within the same browser session, disappear when the browser session ends, and are also removed when you reject or withdraw analytics.

Website fonts (locally hosted)

The font files used by the website are generated and hosted as part of azgs.nl. Loading the typography therefore does not create a separate browser request to Google Fonts.

WhatsApp (only when you click through)

The website may include a wa.me link to WhatsApp. As long as you do not click this link, WhatsApp does not place cookies via our website. Only when you click the link and are redirected to the WhatsApp environment may Meta/WhatsApp place cookies, under their own terms.

What cookies these are and what they are used for depends on your own settings with WhatsApp and Meta and how you use their services. For more information, see the privacy and cookie statements of WhatsApp and Meta.

Formspree (form processing)

The contact and quotation form is technically processed by Formspree. Opening the contact page does not load a Formspree script, iframe, or tracking pixel. Only when you choose to submit the form does your browser connect directly to Formspree and send the selected form data and necessary technical request information.

AZGS does not use this connection for advertising tracking or profiling. Formspree may use technical measures for security, form processing, and abuse prevention. More information about data processing is available in the privacy policy.

Why these cookies?

We limit the use of cookies to what is strictly necessary or to analytical cookies for which you have given consent. This website places no cookies for advertising purposes, remarketing, or tracking individual browsing behavior across websites.

A connection to Formspree is made only when you submit the form. We use Google Analytics cookies — only with your consent — to measure limited usage statistics that help us improve content and structure. The website fonts are self-hosted and do not require a request to Google Fonts.

Consent

For storage necessary to deliver a service you requested or that has little privacy impact, no prior consent is required under the Dutch Telecommunications Act and ePrivacy rules. This applies to submitting the contact form and to our own local browser preference azgs-consent-v1 that remembers your choice.

For analytical Google Analytics cookies, we ask for prior consent. On your first visit, a cookie banner appears at the bottom of the website asking whether you accept these cookies. Google Analytics is only loaded after you click "Accept"; until you choose, we block these cookies completely.

Changing your cookie preferences

You can change your choice at any time. The permanent “Cookie settings” option in the footer reopens the cookie banner without immediately changing your current choice. Choose “Decline” there to stop measurement or “Accept” to allow analytics. You can also use one of the buttons below:

The first two buttons set your preference immediately and store it locally as azgs-consent-v1. When you decline or withdraw, the website stops new analytics events, removes the GA4 script, and attempts to delete its own GA4 cookies. The website remains fully usable when you decline. “Choose again” also stops analytics, removes the stored preference, and immediately displays the cookie banner again.

Disabling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser. Note that some parts of the website may work less well if you disable all cookies. Below are general instructions for the most common browsers:

  • Chrome: open the menu, go to "Settings" > "Privacy and security" > "Cookies and other site data" and adjust your cookie settings there.
  • Firefox: open the menu, choose "Settings" > "Privacy & Security" and set how Firefox should handle cookies under "Cookies and Site Data".
  • Safari (macOS and iOS): go to "Preferences" or "Settings" > "Privacy" and manage cookies and website data there.
  • Microsoft Edge: open the menu, go to "Settings" > "Cookies and site permissions" and choose how you want to handle cookies.

For more detailed instructions, please see your browser vendor's help or support pages.
