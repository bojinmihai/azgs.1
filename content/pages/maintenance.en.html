From request to work order

First assess, then agree clearly

A request is not yet an accepted work order. We assess the available information, access to the work zone, the desired planning and whether specialist work is required. We then record what AZGS will perform and what remains outside the scope.

  • 1. RequestLocation, contact, situation, urgency and available photographs or documents.
  • 2. AssessmentReview of information, access, scope and the need for a site visit.
  • 3. AgreementConfirmation of work, planning, pricing method, materials and responsibilities.
  • 4. ExecutionExecution and feedback in line with the accepted quotation or work order.

Maintenance for buildings in use

Maintenance in a home, restaurant, hotel, office, shop or managed location requires clear boundaries. We therefore agree the technical work, any restoration of the immediate work zone and coordination with other parties in advance.

Maintenance service area

Assessed per address from Woerden

For maintenance, we use two alternative practical outer limits: no more than 50 kilometres from Woerden, or an estimated journey of approximately one hour based on the assessed route and traffic conditions. We check which limit and route are appropriate for each request.

That distance and journey time determine only whether an address may fall within the maintenance service area. They are not a response, dispatch, arrival or repair time and do not guarantee availability or acceptance of an assignment.

Ways of working together

One request, planned maintenance or multiple locations

These are starting points for a suitable quotation, not standard packages. Availability, work and planning are always confirmed in writing.

  • One-off interventionFor B2C and B2B: one defined fault, repair or restoration order at one location.
  • Planned maintenanceFor B2B: agreed activities, locations, frequency and planning windows in a maintenance schedule.
  • Multiple locationsFor B2B: a framework arrangement with a location register, authorised requesters and identifiable work orders per location.
  • TermSet per agreement with a clear end date. Continuation takes place only under a new written agreement.

Included and excluded

Only items stated in the work order form part of the work

This keeps expectations verifiable and makes clear where additional approval or another specialist is required.

  • May be includedAssessment of supplied information, an agreed visit, accessible work, a feasible temporary measure and finishing of the immediate work zone as described.
  • Not automatically includedUnlimited reactive work, preventive maintenance, major replacements, destructive investigation or hidden and additional work.
  • Specialist workDesign, calculations, certification, commissioning and regulated inspections only when explicitly agreed with a suitable party.
  • Other partiesOverall coordination, occupant communication and restoration outside the immediate work zone are included only when stated in the quotation.

Practical conditions

Planning, materials, access, reporting and invoicing

The specific arrangements are recorded in the quotation, work order or maintenance schedule.

  • ScheduleWork is planned within windows agreed for each order. Work outside such a window requires separate confirmation of availability, scope and costs.
  • MaterialsEach order states who supplies materials and what costs apply. Materials, consumables, delivery and purchasing are not automatically included.
  • AccessThe client provides timely, safe and authorised access, an available contact and relevant site and installation information.
  • AuthorisationAdditional work and replacements are performed after approval under the authorisation threshold agreed in advance.
  • ReportingA short confirmation, intervention record or periodic overview can be agreed. Content, frequency, photographs and costs are determined in advance.
  • InvoicingFixed price or time and materials, per order or period. For multiple locations, invoices can be issued per order or combined with clear references.

Business · Multiple locations

Maintenance arrangements that remain identifiable per location

A framework arrangement can be developed for property managers, hospitality businesses, hotels, offices and retail. It covers only the confirmed locations, authorised requesters, access arrangements and reporting and invoice references.

  • Location registerPer location: address, contact, access, relevant characteristics and agreed activities.
  • Work orderEach individual repair falls under an accepted work order or a category expressly included in the framework arrangement.
  • ReferencesLocation, order number and, where applicable, PO or cost centre can be included in reporting and invoicing.
  • No implicit reservationA framework arrangement does not automatically cover every location, exclusivity, fixed capacity or a guaranteed response time.
