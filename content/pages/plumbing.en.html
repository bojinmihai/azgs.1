How we work

From assessment to delivery

Four steps to carefully complete your plumbing installation.

1

Assessment and planning

We start with an assessment of your goals and the existing situation in the bathroom or restroom. We review the current piping, drains, and fixtures and discuss which parts should be replaced or newly installed. That gives us a clear plan for the work.

2

Prep and removal

Where needed, we remove existing fixtures and prepare the space for the new installation. We work carefully to limit damage to walls and floors. Any adjustments to piping or drains happen in this phase.

3

Installation and connection

Next, we install the new fixtures: toilets, sinks, showers, tubs, and faucets. We ensure solid mounting, correct connection to water supply and drainage, and check for leaks. Where needed, we finish connections cleanly with sealant and the right connection materials.

4

Testing and delivery

After installation, we test every function: water pressure, flow, and drainage. We clean up the work area and walk through the bathroom or restroom with you. We explain what was done and what to watch for in regular use.

Materials and quality

For plumbing installations, we use solid piping material, fasteners, and fittings that suit the fixtures and your existing system. We focus on a balance of reliability and easy maintenance. By mounting carefully and thoroughly testing connections, we reduce the chance of leaks and deliver a clean finish in your bathroom or restroom.

What determines the scope of your plumbing project?

The work depends on existing water and waste pipes, fixture dimensions, and the intended position. Photos, dimensions, and product details help us determine whether connections can remain or need to be moved.

  • Existing or new water and waste connection points
  • Dimensions and specifications of the selected fixtures
  • Access to pipework in walls, floors, or service ducts
  • Tile, sealant, or wall repair required after installation

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about plumbing

Do you install just a new faucet or toilet?

Yes, we handle both complete bathrooms and smaller jobs — like replacing a toilet or installing a new faucet. When you first get in touch, we discuss the scope and set up a suitable appointment for your home in the Utrecht region.

Do I buy the fixtures myself, or can you help?

You can pick out fixtures yourself at suppliers, but we're also happy to weigh in on practical aspects like sizing and connections. When in doubt, we discuss the product choice with you so it's clear the fixtures will fit the existing or modified installation.

What if a piping problem shows up during installation?

Sometimes hidden problems with piping or drains surface during the work. In that case, we first discuss with you what additional steps are needed and what the possible costs are. We don't take extra measures without clearly communicating this up front.

Do you coordinate with other trades on bathroom renovations?

Yes, on larger renovations we coordinate work with other parties involved, such as tile setters or contractors. AZ Grand Solutions can handle both plumbing and other finishing work, so scheduling lines up better.
