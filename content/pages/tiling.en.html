How we work

From assessment to ready-to-use

Four steps to carefully complete your tiling project.

1

Site visit and tile selection

We start with an on-site visit and inventory the rooms and substrates. Together with you, we review tile size and type, layout patterns, and any special requirements — a walk-in shower or accent walls, for example. That gives us a clear plan for the tiling.

2

Substrate and waterproofing

Next, we prep the substrate by leveling or reinforcing where needed. In wet rooms we apply waterproof layers and pay attention to slope and drainage. This forms the base for durable tiling without unnecessary risk of leaks.

3

Setting and grouting tiles

We set the tiles in the agreed pattern, with attention to clean lines and transitions. Cutting around corners, niches, and drains we handle precisely. After setting, we grout the tiles and seal joints and transitions.

4

Delivery and ready-to-use

When we're done, we clean the tiling, tidy up the work area, and walk through the result with you. We explain first-time use, cleaning, and maintenance so you can start using your new bathroom, kitchen, or floor with confidence.

Materials and quality

We use suitable adhesives, leveling compounds, waterproof systems, and grout products that fit the space and tile type. Tile size and weight place specific demands on the substrate — we account for that in the buildup. By working carefully and using the right materials, we deliver tiling with a clean finish and solid longevity.

Preparation determines the finished result

Flatness, moisture exposure, tile size, and pattern determine the preparation required. Photos, dimensions, and tile specifications help us assess cuts and junctions in advance.

  • Waterproofing in showers and other wet zones
  • Tile layout around corners, niches, and fixtures

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about tiling

Can you also remove the old tiles?

Yes, we can remove existing tiles and rebuild the substrate. This can add time and work — leveling, for example. During the on-site visit we discuss what's needed and include it in the quote for your tiling project in the Utrecht region.

Do you only do bathrooms?

No, we handle tiling in bathrooms, kitchens, restrooms, and other rooms like hallways or living rooms. Whether a small restroom or a full bathroom renovation, we tailor our approach to the space and your goals.

How do you make the tiling watertight?

In wet zones we use waterproof systems behind and under the tiling, combined with proper drainage. Transitions and penetrations are carefully sealed. That reduces the risk of leaks and water damage, provided the space is also used and ventilated properly.

Do you work with large-format tiles?

Yes, we work with both standard sizes and large-format tiles. With large formats we pay extra attention to substrate, adhesive choice, and installation method. We discuss this up front so it's clear what's technically feasible in your space.

Where do you work?

This is the private residential scope, not the separate building-maintenance service area. Requests from Utrecht, Amersfoort, Nieuwegein, Zeist, Houten, IJsselstein, Gouda, Alphen aan den Rijn, Hilversum, Veenendaal and Bunnik, among others, are assessed by location.
