Article 3 - Application, supply and order of precedence

  1. These terms apply to consumer agreements to which AZGS declares them applicable before or when the Agreement is concluded. They do not apply where the Client acts in the course of a profession or business.
  2. AZGS supplies the applicable version before or when the Agreement is concluded, on paper or electronically in a way that allows the Client to store and consult the text later. Merely referring to a web page does not replace such supply where the law requires information on a durable medium.
  3. If provisions conflict, project-specific terms in the quotation or Agreement accepted by the parties take precedence over these terms. A provision detrimental to the Client does not apply where mandatory consumer law prohibits it.
  4. If a provision is unclear, the reasonable interpretation most favourable to the consumer applies. A provision that is void or annulled does not affect the remaining provisions.

Article 4 - Quotation, information and formation

  1. Unless it states otherwise, a quotation is valid for fourteen days. Obvious writing, calculation or measurement errors do not bind AZGS where the Client should reasonably have understood that an error had been made.
  2. The quotation states at least the agreed work, relevant assumptions and exclusions, the price or calculation method, VAT, any additional costs, payment stages and intended schedule insofar as these can be determined at that time.
  3. An Agreement is formed by acceptance of the quotation, signature of an agreement, or when AZGS starts performance at the Client's express request. An acceptance that changes the quotation binds AZGS only after AZGS accepts that change.
  4. For a distance or off-premises agreement, AZGS provides the information required by law before the Agreement is concluded and a confirmation on a durable medium afterwards. The contact form on this website is for making an enquiry only and does not itself conclude an Agreement.
  5. Insofar as the Agreement concerns construction of a new home for a private Client, AZGS supplies before conclusion, in writing and unambiguously, the legally required information about whether the relevant risks have financial cover and, if cover exists, the characteristics required by law. This clause does not confirm any insurance, guarantee or warranty; the project-specific information is decisive.

Article 5 - Withdrawal period for distance or off-premises agreements

  1. Where the law grants a right of withdrawal, the Client may withdraw from the Agreement within fourteen days without giving a reason. For a service, that period starts on the day after the Agreement is concluded. AZGS supplies the legally required information about exercising this right.
  2. The Client may withdraw by any unequivocal statement to AZGS, for example by email or by using the model form in Annex A. If the Client withdraws through an online function, AZGS promptly confirms receipt on a durable medium.
  3. AZGS starts during the withdrawal period only at the Client's express request. If the Client then withdraws, the Client owes only a proportionate amount for performance up to the time of withdrawal, provided the statutory information and consent requirements have been met.
  4. The right of withdrawal for a fully performed service ends only if performance began with the Client's prior express consent and the Client acknowledged that the right would end after full performance, insofar as the law permits.
  5. For a visit specifically requested by the Client for urgent repairs or maintenance, the statutory exception from the right of withdrawal applies only to the urgently requested work. Additional services or goods not necessary for that urgent work do not automatically fall within the exception.
  6. Insofar as the special statutory regime for construction of a home for a consumer applies, the statutory written-form requirement and three-day reflection period also apply. This is separate from any right of withdrawal for a distance or off-premises agreement.

  4. Invoices are payable within fourteen days of the invoice date unless another reasonable period is agreed in Writing. A substantive objection should be raised promptly; statutory rights of suspension or set-off remain available.
  5. If payment is late, AZGS charges statutory interest and extrajudicial collection costs to a consumer only after the legally required free payment reminder has been correctly sent and its payment period has expired. Collection costs will not exceed the statutory maximum.

Article 8 - Client duties and AZGS's duty to warn

  1. The Client supplies in good time the information and decisions reasonably needed by AZGS and provides agreed access to the work site, provided that it is safely accessible.

  5. AZGS may stop the affected Work in an unsafe situation. AZGS informs the Client as soon as possible about the reason, necessary measures and foreseeable price and scheduling consequences.

  3. Delay caused by a circumstance attributable to the Client may lead to a reasonable schedule adjustment and demonstrable costs after AZGS has informed the Client in good time.
  4. The Client's statutory rights in the event of late or defective performance remain applicable.

  1. The Client reports a discovered defect within a reasonable time after discovering it or after the Client reasonably should have discovered it. An email with photographs and a description helps prompt assessment, but a statutory notice is not subject to stricter formal requirements than the law permits.
  2. For a contract concerning a constructed work (bouwwerk), AZGS is liable for defects not discovered at completion unless those defects cannot be attributed to AZGS. Section 7:758(4) of the Dutch Civil Code is not varied to the detriment of the consumer.
  3. In principle, the Client gives AZGS a reasonable opportunity to investigate and, if AZGS is responsible, repair a defect within a reasonable period. This does not apply where it cannot reasonably be required of the Client, for example in a demonstrable emergency.
  4. A complaint suspends payment only insofar as the law permits and the amount withheld is reasonably proportionate to the alleged failure.
  5. Statutory complaint, expiry and limitation periods remain applicable. These terms do not impose a shorter expiry period on consumers.
