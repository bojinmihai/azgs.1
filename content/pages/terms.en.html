Article 1 — Definitions

In these terms and conditions, the following definitions apply:

  1. AZGS: AZ Grand Solutions, registered at Alpenstraat 12, 3446 DN Woerden, entered in the Dutch Chamber of Commerce under number 42064891, acting as contractor under these terms.
  2. Client: the natural person not acting in the course of a profession or business who enters into or wishes to enter into an agreement with AZGS (consumer).
  3. Work: all activities to be performed by AZGS in the areas of finishing (including drywall, interior painting, parquet, tiling) and installations (including plumbing, heating, underfloor heating, and electrical), including any materials, as described in the agreement.
  4. Agreement: the agreement entered into between AZGS and the client for the contracting of work or provision of services, as well as any amendment or addition thereto.
  5. Quote: any written or digital offer from AZGS to the client to enter into an agreement.

Article 3 — Quotes and offers

  1. All quotes and offers from AZGS are without obligation unless the quote states a period for acceptance.

  4. The quote contains a clear description of the work and — as far as possible — an indication of the materials to be used. The description is sufficiently detailed to allow the client to properly assess the offer.

Article 4 — Formation of the agreement

  1. The agreement takes effect when the client has accepted the AZGS quote in writing or by email, or when the parties have signed a written agreement.
  2. If the client's acceptance differs from the offer in the quote, the agreement only takes effect once AZGS has agreed to the deviation in writing.
  3. Verbal agreements and commitments bind AZGS only to the extent that AZGS has confirmed them in writing or by email.

Article 5 — Prices and payment

  1. Unless expressly stated otherwise, the prices stated in the quote or agreement for consumers include VAT and other government-imposed charges.
  2. Payment must be made within 14 days of the invoice date, in the manner specified by AZGS, unless a different payment term has been agreed in writing.
  3. For larger works or projects, AZGS is entitled to require a deposit or payment in installments — for example, an advance payment, one or more partial invoices during execution, and a final payment on delivery. This is set out in the quote or agreement.
  4. If unforeseen circumstances arise during the work that require additional work or costs (for example due to hidden defects or necessary adjustments), AZGS will inform the client as soon as possible. Any price adjustments are handled as additional work under Article 7.
  5. If the client does not pay within the agreed term, they are in default — after a free payment reminder and after a further reasonable term has elapsed. From that point, statutory interest may be claimed and reasonable extrajudicial collection costs may be charged, in accordance with the statutory consumer rules.

  3. AZGS will use sound materials suited to the nature of the work and the agreed quality level. If the client wishes to use specific brands or materials, this is only applied when expressly agreed; any additional costs may be passed on to the client.
  4. The client ensures that AZGS has timely access to the site where the work is carried out, that the site is safe and accessible, and that the required connections (such as electricity and water) are available, unless otherwise agreed.

  1. If during execution of the work it becomes apparent that proper performance requires additional or modified work, AZGS and the client will consult on it. Such changes may affect the agreed price and timelines.

Article 8 — Delivery

  1. The work is considered delivered when AZGS has informed the client that the work is complete and the client has approved it, or when the client has effectively taken the work into use.

  1. The statutory rules on conformity and non-conformity apply to the work and materials delivered: what is delivered must meet the agreement and the legitimate expectations the client may have of it based on that agreement.
  2. If defects come to light after delivery that the client could not reasonably have discovered at delivery (hidden defects), the client must report them to AZGS within a reasonable period after discovering them or after they should reasonably have been discovered. AZGS is then given a reasonable period to remedy the defects.

    4. failure to follow instructions or maintenance guidelines, or not following them correctly;
    5. external causes, such as water damage, fire, leaks, or other circumstances beyond AZGS's control.

Article 10 — Liability

  1. AZGS is only liable to the client for damage that is the direct and immediate result of an attributable shortcoming in the performance of the agreement, or of a tort for which AZGS can be held liable.
  2. Indirect and consequential damage — including but not limited to business damage, loss of revenue or profit, missed savings, and damage from business interruption — is not eligible for compensation, to the extent permitted by Dutch consumer law.
  3. Except where mandatory law provides otherwise, the liability of AZGS per event or series of related events is limited to the invoice amount that AZGS has charged (or would charge) for the part of the work concerned, provided that this limitation may not affect the mandatory statutory rights of the consumer.

Article 11 — Force majeure

  1. Force majeure is any circumstance that temporarily or permanently prevents AZGS from performing the agreement and that cannot be attributed to AZGS — such as, among other things: extreme weather, fire, flooding, war, terrorism, pandemics, government measures, strikes, disruptions in the supply of materials, power outages, and other serious disruptions at AZGS or its suppliers.
  2. In the event of force majeure, the obligation to perform the work is suspended for the duration of the force majeure. AZGS will inform the client as soon as possible about the force-majeure situation and its expected duration.
  3. If the force-majeure situation lasts longer than reasonably acceptable, both AZGS and the client are entitled to terminate the agreement in whole or in part, without either party owing compensation, to the extent permitted under consumer law.
  4. To the extent AZGS has already performed part of the work by the time the force-majeure event begins, AZGS is entitled to invoice this part separately.

  1. AZGS offers a 24/7 emergency service for urgent faults and incidents, including water leaks, heating faults, electrical faults, and drain unblocking in the service area (Utrecht region, within a 60-km radius of Woerden).
  2. When the emergency service is called in, AZGS will make every effort to arrive on-site as quickly as possible, subject to available capacity and conditions. An exact response time cannot be guaranteed.
  3. Separate rates may apply to the emergency service, including call-out costs, evening or night rates, and weekend or public-holiday surcharges. These rates are explained to the client before or at the time the emergency assignment is accepted — verbally or, where possible, in writing or by email.
  4. The client is obliged to pay for the services provided and materials used by AZGS in connection with the emergency service, in accordance with the applicable rates and the specification provided by AZGS.

Article 13 — Cancellation by the client

  1. The client may cancel a scheduled appointment or assignment. The client should notify AZGS of a cancellation as soon as possible, preferably in writing or by email.
  2. If the client cancels an assignment after AZGS has already incurred demonstrable costs — for example, for materials already purchased or preparatory work — AZGS may charge these reasonable costs to the client. AZGS will specify these costs as concretely as possible.

  4. Cancellation of the emergency service is only possible as long as AZGS has not yet departed for the location. If AZGS is already en route or has performed work, AZGS may charge the costs incurred and reasonable compensation for time already spent.
