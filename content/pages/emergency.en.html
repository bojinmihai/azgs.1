Safety first

Immediate danger requires the competent emergency service

In case of fire, a gas smell, serious injury, immediate electrocution risk or another directly dangerous situation, call 112 or the competent fault service first. Do not put yourself at risk or touch a wet electrical installation.

  • LeakOnly if it is safe, close the main or local water valve and limit further water flow.
  • Electrical riskKeep clear of water and live equipment; use 112 or the competent fault service in case of immediate danger.
  • Occupied buildingKeep people away from the affected area and follow the location's safety procedure.

Urgent-request service area

The route and traffic conditions determine the outer limit

For an urgent request from Woerden, we use two alternative practical outer limits: no more than 50 kilometres, or an estimated drive of approximately 40 minutes based on the assessed route and traffic conditions.

This is solely a criterion for the potential service area. The 40 minutes are not a response, dispatch or arrival time and do not constitute an SLA. Availability, acceptance, scope and planning are always assessed and confirmed separately.

We assess an urgent business request only when it concerns a project or installation previously carried out by AZ Grand Solutions. This restriction likewise gives no entitlement to availability or acceptance.

Scope before work

A temporary measure and permanent repair are separate agreements

Following assessment, an assignment may be limited to making the situation safer or limiting damage. For private and separate maintenance requests, diagnosis, permanent repair and restoration of walls, ceilings, tiles, sealant or painting are included only when expressly confirmed. For a business emergency request, the potential scope remains limited to the sanitary installation or pipework, heating installation or ventilation installation previously carried out by AZGS to which the request relates.

Frequently asked questions

Clarity for an urgent request

Is AZ Grand Solutions always available for urgent work?

No permanent availability is promised. Call for a current assessment. We separately confirm whether attendance is possible and when a next step could take place.

When can someone attend?

This can only be assessed after receiving information about the location, situation, access and available planning. A phone call or form does not provide a guaranteed arrival time.

Can I use the form for an urgent request?

The form is suitable for a non-immediate callback or maintenance request and is not monitored continuously. Call if the situation is currently urgent.

Will the issue always be solved in one visit?

No. Access, diagnosis, parts, materials, drying time or specialist work may require a temporary measure or follow-up assignment.

Which costs apply?

Before acceptance, we discuss the available pricing basis, such as a fixed price or time and materials, plus relevant travel, parking and material costs. Unforeseen additional work is discussed first.

Does AZGS handle the insurance claim?

No. The client discusses coverage with their own insurer. A work description or photographic record can be provided only if agreed in advance and permitted at the location.
