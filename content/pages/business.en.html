Service area and urgent requests

Project delivery, clearly separated from maintenance and urgent work

For B2B projects, we assess locations from Woerden across a broader project area. The confirmed orientation points are Breda, Tilburg and Eindhoven to the south; Purmerend and Beverwijk to the north; The Hague, Rotterdam and Leiden to the west; and Lelystad and Zwolle to the east. These places do not guarantee acceptance or full coverage: project size, programme, travel time and capacity are always assessed in advance. Other locations are considered only after a separate assessment.

We assess an urgent business request only when it concerns an installation previously executed by AZGS within that project. Availability, response and possible follow-up are confirmed for each situation; no public SLA or guaranteed arrival time applies.
