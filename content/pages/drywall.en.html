How we work

From assessment to delivery

Four steps to carefully complete your drywall project.

1

Site visit and scope

We start with a site visit in the Utrecht region to inspect the existing situation and walk through what you want done. We measure walls and ceilings, discuss whether insulation is needed, and determine which type of metal-stud framing and drywall is right for the job.

2

Framing and installation

Next, we set the metal-stud framing and mount the drywall carefully. We focus on a flat result, clean joins, and enough fastening points. If needed, we add insulation in the walls or ceilings for extra comfort.

3

Seam finishing

Once all drywall is up, we finish seams and screw holes with joint compound and tape. We make sure the surface is ready for paint, plaster, or tile, with a clean finish. We leave the work area tidy when we're done.

4

Coordination with other trades

On projects that also involve painting, tiling, or installations, we coordinate the work carefully with the other trades. That prevents delays and keeps every step on schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about drywall

How long does drywall work take in my home?

The duration depends on how many walls and ceilings are involved and any extras like insulation. For smaller projects in the Utrecht region we're often finished within a few days; larger projects take longer. During the on-site visit we give you a realistic timeline.

Can you install insulation behind the drywall?

Yes, we can add insulation in furring walls and ceilings before the drywall goes up. That helps with sound transmission or heat loss. We advise on which insulation fits your situation and integrate it into the metal-stud framing during installation.

Are the walls ready to paint right after installation?

After the drywall is hung and finished, walls and ceilings are generally ready for the next step. Sometimes a final fine-sanding or skim coat is needed for the best result. We'll discuss whether we should handle the painting or tiling as well, so everything lines up cleanly.

Do you only work in Utrecht city?

No, we work within roughly a 60-kilometer radius of Woerden. That means beyond Utrecht, we're active in Amersfoort, Nieuwegein, Zeist, Houten, IJsselstein, Gouda, Alphen aan den Rijn, Hilversum, Veenendaal, and Bunnik, among others. Travel time stays limited and scheduling stays practical.

What does drywall installation cost per square meter?

The price per square meter depends on the buildup: framing, type of drywall, insulation, and finish level. We inspect the situation on-site in the Utrecht region and then prepare a clear, itemized estimate. You receive a no-obligation quote where the scope and costs are laid out plainly.
