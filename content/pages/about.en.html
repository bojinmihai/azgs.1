What we stand for

Five principles that show up in every project

At AZ Grand Solutions, the focus is technical reliability, clear scope, correct planning and professional delivery. You know what is being executed, who is responsible and how the result will be handed over.

Direct personal phone contact

When you call, you get direct personal contact with someone who listens to your situation and brings in the right specialist. No long menus and no unnecessary transfers between departments — just a clear explanation and an honest assessment of what's needed.

Respect for your home

We treat your home with respect, as if we were working in our own house. That means we work carefully, protect floors and furniture where needed, and handle your belongings properly. You experience as little disruption as possible during the work.

A commitment is a commitment

We take scheduling and commitments seriously. Before work starts, we define the scope, timing and important dependencies. If something unexpected appears during execution, we discuss it properly before moving forward.

Delivered cleanly

For us, a project is complete when the technical execution has been checked and the work area is left representative. We review connections, the finish around the work area and the agreed delivery points, so the result is professionally usable.

Transparent pricing

We aim for transparent pricing so you understand up front how the costs break down. We explain what work is needed, what's included, and what optional extras exist. No hidden costs — an honest explanation, without unpleasant surprises afterward.

Agreements and assurance

Clear agreements before, during and after execution

We do not make blanket warranty promises without conditions. The scope, materials and any additional warranties are recorded for each assignment, so you know in advance what you can rely on.
