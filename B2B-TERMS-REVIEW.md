## Required use with every B2B quotation

Do not rely only on a footer link or publication on the website. Supply the applicable version before or when the agreement is formed, preferably as a PDF attached to the quotation, and retain evidence of delivery and acceptance.

Recommended Dutch quotation clause:

> Op deze offerte en de daaruit voortvloeiende overeenkomst zijn uitsluitend de Algemene voorwaarden voor zakelijke opdrachtgevers (B2B) van AZ Grand Solutions vof, versie 1.0 van 2 september 2026, van toepassing. Deze voorwaarden zijn vóór aanvaarding als bijlage verstrekt en kunnen door de opdrachtgever worden opgeslagen. Algemene (inkoop)voorwaarden van de opdrachtgever worden uitdrukkelijk van de hand gewezen.

Recommended English quotation clause:

> The General Terms and Conditions for Business Clients (B2B) of AZ Grand Solutions vof, version 1.0 dated 2 September 2026, apply exclusively to this quotation and the resulting agreement. These terms have been supplied as an attachment before acceptance and can be stored by the Client. The Client's general purchasing or other terms are expressly rejected. The Dutch version is legally binding.

If a Client has already referred to named purchasing conditions, reject those conditions specifically by name and version in the quotation or reply; a generic rejection may not be sufficient in a battle-of-forms dispute.

## Project contract point required by the Wkb

The general terms deliberately do not depart from Section 7:758(4) of the Dutch Civil Code. For a structure, any departure to the detriment of a professional Client must be expressly and project-specifically included in the Agreement itself. A reference to a clause in general conditions alone is insufficient. Do not add such a deviation to a quotation template without Dutch construction-law advice.

## Business choices for Dutch legal review

1. Confirm that the liability cap — the net invoice value of the affected part of the assignment — matches AZGS's liability insurance and the expected project values.
2. Confirm the fourteen-day payment period and the collection-cost clause (15%, minimum €150).
3. Confirm the five-Business-Day completion inspection period and ten-Business-Day defect notice period for the types and size of projects AZGS accepts.
4. Confirm the scope exclusions in Article 5 against the standard quotation template, particularly permits, access equipment, fire stopping, commissioning, inspections and as-built documentation.
5. Specify project-specific insurance, certification, VCA, warranty, retention, bank-guarantee and completion-file requirements in the quotation where applicable.
6. Decide whether to file the final Dutch terms with KVK or a court. Filing is not mandatory and does not validate their content, but can help prove which version existed.
7. Have a Dutch construction-law lawyer review the Dutch binding text before first contractual use. The English version should then be checked against the approved Dutch version.
