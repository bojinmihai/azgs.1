## Operational scope reflected in version 1.1

- B2B installation projects are limited to sanitary installations and pipework, thermal/heating installations (including underfloor heating as a thermal installation), and ventilation installations.
- Other finishing work, drywall, electrical work and heat-pump work are not part of the B2B project offer.
- B2B urgent work is assessed only for an AZGS project or installation previously carried out by AZGS and only within the relevant sanitary, heating or ventilation installation.
- Separate maintenance work can be agreed only with a concrete scope; it does not enlarge the B2B installation-project offer.
- A Dutch construction-law lawyer must specifically verify Articles 1, 4 and 5 against this operational scope before contractual use. These changes are not presented as legally approved.

## Required use with every B2B quotation

Do not rely only on a footer link or publication on the website. Supply the applicable version before or when the agreement is formed, preferably as a PDF attached to the quotation, and retain evidence of delivery and acceptance.

Recommended Dutch quotation clause:

> Op deze offerte en de overeenkomst die door aanvaarding daarvan tot stand komt, zijn de "Algemene voorwaarden voor zakelijke opdrachtgevers (B2B) van AZ Grand Solutions vof", versie 1.1, d.d. 3 september 2026, van toepassing. Deze voorwaarden zijn vóór het sluiten van de overeenkomst als PDF bij deze offerte gevoegd, zodat opdrachtgever ze kan opslaan en later raadplegen. Algemene inkoop- of andere voorwaarden van opdrachtgever worden uitdrukkelijk van de hand gewezen.

Recommended English quotation clause:

> The "General terms and conditions for business clients (B2B) of AZ Grand Solutions vof", version 1.1 dated 3 September 2026, apply to this quotation and the agreement formed by its acceptance. The binding Dutch terms and the informational English translation have been attached as PDFs before conclusion of the agreement so that the Client can store and consult them later. The Client's general purchasing or other terms are expressly rejected. The Dutch text is legally controlling.

If a Client has already referred to named purchasing conditions, reject those conditions specifically by name and version in the quotation or reply; a generic rejection may not be sufficient in a battle-of-forms dispute.

## Project contract point required by the Wkb

The general terms deliberately do not depart from Section 7:758(4) of the Dutch Civil Code. For a structure, any departure to the detriment of a professional Client must be expressly and project-specifically included in the Agreement itself. A reference to a clause in general conditions alone is insufficient. Do not add such a deviation to a quotation template without Dutch construction-law advice.

## Business choices for Dutch legal review

1. Confirm that the liability cap — the net invoice value of the affected part of the assignment — matches AZGS's liability insurance and the expected project values.
2. Confirm the fourteen-day payment period and the collection-cost clause (15%, minimum €150).
3. Confirm the five-Business-Day completion inspection period and ten-Business-Day defect notice period for the types and size of projects AZGS accepts.
4. Confirm the scope exclusions in Article 5 against the standard quotation template, particularly permits, access equipment, fire stopping, commissioning and inspections; confirm project-specific `as-built` and Section 7:757a completion-file requirements separately.
5. Specify project-specific insurance, certification, VCA, warranty, retention, bank-guarantee and completion-file requirements in the quotation where applicable.
6. Decide whether to file the final Dutch terms with KVK or a court. Filing is not mandatory and does not validate their content, but can help prove which version existed.
7. Have a Dutch construction-law lawyer review the Dutch binding text before first contractual use. The English version should then be checked against the approved Dutch version. See the complete checklist in `AZGS-LEGAL-REVIEW.md`.
